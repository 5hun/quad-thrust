The hull and rudders use the smooth grey metal's albedo and a restrained normal
map, tinted as painted racing bodywork. The canopy uses a separately authored procedural tinted-glass DataTexture
(128 × 128, created in src/standard-machine.ts), with no third-party source.
Lights are untextured.
Images are bundled locally; no runtime downloads are required.
